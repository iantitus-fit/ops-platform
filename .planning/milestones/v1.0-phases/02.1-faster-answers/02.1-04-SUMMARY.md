# Phase 02.1 Plan 04: Every Reply a Waiting Customer Can Get Summary

**Owner folders now carry the partial, check-in, added-info, complaint and optional discount replies plus four price leads, all checked at sync like the holding reply, with old live versions falling back to the holding reply and check-in alerts timed by the owner's right_away list.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 7 min
- **Started:** 2026-10-14T20:01:43Z
- **Completed:** 2026-10-14T20:08:24Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 13

## Accomplishments

- A folder without a complaint reply, partial holding reply, check-in reply or added-info reply cannot go live. The error reads `rules/replies.md: <field> is missing, and it is required`.
- Price leads are required exactly when a service file has price sentences or a default, and the sync names that service. A folder with no price sentences still syncs without them.
- Every reply and every lead runs the price, discount, promise and never_say checks plus the fill-in whitelist. Only `discount_reply` skips the discount check.
- `FixedReplies` gained five reply accessors and `priceLead()`. Each reply falls back to the holding reply when a version compiled before this phase lacks the field, and `priceLead()` returns null so the price sentence goes alone.
- The example `rules/replies.md` is rewritten: the holding reply promises a place in line (`Thanks. I've passed this to Mike and you're on his list. He'll get back to you ...`) and every new reply and lead is in the customer-first voice. No exclamation point or em dash anywhere in the file.
- `check_in` is an `AlertReason` the owner times in `rules/sending.md`; the example lists it under `right_away`. `RaiseOwnerAlert` is untouched.

## Decisions Made

- `fixedTextError()` gained a skip list and a display label rather than a second copy of the check, so a lead reads `price_leads same makes a promise (guaranteed)` while its field path stays `price_leads.same`.
- `replyTextErrors()` now holds the shared fill-in, check-in-time and code-check path for every reply and lead, replacing the inline loop in `repliesErrors()`.
- A present `price_leads` that is not a map is left to the `array` rule; only a map is checked key by key.
- Followed plan wording for all example replies; the body of `replies.md` was rewritten in plain words and explains each reply, the fill-ins, `check_in_hours` and the sync checks.
