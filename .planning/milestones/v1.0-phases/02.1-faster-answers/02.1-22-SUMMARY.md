# Phase 02.1 Plan 22: Business-Wide Alerts and safety_check_down Summary

**The owner's phone can now be told about the business itself: a new always-urgent `safety_check_down` reason, stored in `owner_alerts` with no conversation, texted to every user with a phone at any hour with the dashboard link last.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 6 min
- **Started:** 2026-10-16T21:48Z
- **Completed:** 2026-10-16T21:54Z
- **Tasks:** 2 (a tracer and one TDD task)
- **Files modified:** 8

## Accomplishments

- Migration makes `owner_alerts.conversation_id` nullable, keeping its foreign key; it migrates, rolls back and re-migrates cleanly on SQLite.
- `AlertReason::SafetyCheckDown` ("Safety check is down"), urgent in `RaiseOwnerAlert::isUrgent()` like `life_safety`, whatever the folder's `right_away` list says. The folder may list it (it syncs) or leave it off (nothing changes).
- `RaiseOwnerAlert::forBusiness()` stores one alert per user with a phone (or one failed "No phone on file" alert when nobody has one), with no conversation or message, urgent, due now, and sends it at once. It never dedupes.
- `OwnerAlertText::forBusiness()` writes `{plain words}: {why}`, a blank line, then `Filament::getUrl()`. It quotes no customer words (T-02.1-48).
- `SendOwnerAlert::addToTimeline()` returns early when the alert has no conversation, so a business alert writes no customer timeline entry and reports no error whether it is sent, skipped or failed.
