# Roadmap: Ops Platform

## Overview

Ops Platform is a client-owned operating system where the business runs from a folder. The committed roadmap (v1, SPEC Sprints 0 to 2) takes it from a fresh scaffold to the headline outcome in three phases. Phase 1 makes the folder drive the app: a validated, versioned sync, every way a lead can arrive landing on one timeline, a Filament dashboard, and customer cards rendered into `records/`. Phase 2 puts an AI text front desk on that timeline, with a send-or-draft gate enforced in code and the model chosen in a file. Phase 3 finishes the conversation with booking, reminders and owner alerts, and turns on the activation gate: scripted customers walk-test every folder change before it goes live. Sprints 3 to 6 (quote to invoice, voice, automations, settings by conversation and connectors) are parked in the Backlog and are pulled by a real client or job conversation, one at a time.

### Phase 2: Text Front Desk

**Goal**: A customer who texts gets a reply that moves the conversation forward without the owner, from an agent defined in a file, and nothing risky is ever sent without the owner.
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: AGNT-01, AGNT-02, AGNT-03, AGNT-04, GATE-01, GATE-02, GATE-03, GATE-04, TXT-01, INFRA-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A customer who texts through the fake channel (for example from `/dev/chat`) gets a reply from the agent in the same conversation; the first reply says it is an AI, uses the voice file's sign-off, and never uses a `never_say` phrase; and the agent then asks the matching service's intake questions and the answers show on the timeline and the customer card.
  2. A message involving a price, a promise, a discount or a complaint is never sent: it appears as a draft in the dashboard and the owner is alerted, while intake questions, scheduling and FAQ answers send on their own, even when a scripted model tries to send the draft.
  3. The owner can take over a conversation so the agent stops replying until it is handed back, and can approve, edit and send, or discard a draft from the dashboard; a customer who asks for a person is handed to the `escalate_to` contact.
  4. Changing an agent's provider, model or fallback list is an edit to its `agents/*.md` file that goes live through the normal sync, a broken agent file is rejected with a plain-words message, and when the primary provider fails the agent falls through to its fallback.
  5. The Twilio text driver sits behind the same `TextChannel` interface and is selected by config alone, verified against Twilio-shaped payloads (going live on Ian's test number follows A2P approval and does not gate this phase), and the full Pest suite passes on Postgres in CI as well as on SQLite locally.

**Plans**: 41/41 plans complete; 35 executed, 6 UAT gap-closure plans (02-36 to 02-41) to run (one plan per wave, run in order: one working tree per DEC-01, each plan runs the full suite, and most share the front-desk files or the sprint-1.md walk-through log; re-sliced from 17 on 2026-10-11 so each plan fits its context budget)

### Phase 02.1: Faster Answers (INSERTED)

**Goal:** Routine questions finish without the owner: a price or offer the owner wrote goes out at once, sent by code and never written by the model; an easy answer never waits behind a hard question already with the owner; and a customer who is waiting always hears back, in the owner's words, with only promises the hours can keep.
**Depends on:** Phase 2
**Requirements**: ANSR-01, ANSR-02, ANSR-03, ANSR-04
**Scope** (inserted ahead of the Owner Loop on 2026-10-14 from Phase 2 UAT round 2; see `docs/phase-2.1-owner-input.md` and `.planning/phases/02.1-faster-answers/02.1-CONTEXT.md`):
- Owner-written price sentences per service, keyed to intake answers with a default sentence, picked and sent by code and checked at sync against the file's prices; a price the model writes still holds (02-CONTEXT D-08 stands for model-written replies).
- Owner-written conditional offer sentences, sent while live by date; a warm discount deferral when none is.
- A safe answer sends while a hard draft waits (touches flag, split, label guard); a price draft that a price sentence answers closes as `answered`.
- The partial holding, check-in, added-info and complaint replies; new holding-reply time fill-ins from `hours.md` and an optional check-in time; the example folder's holding reply rewritten customer-first.
- The prompt fix: never mention a question already with the owner.

**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A customer who asks a price for a service with price sentences is asked the keying intake questions, then gets the owner's sentence for their answers (or the default sentence) sent by code; a corrected answer re-sends with the owner's same, higher, lower or changed lead; a price the model writes, or a service with `price_policy: none`, still holds; a price sentence whose amounts do not match its file fails the sync in plain words.
  2. A discount question gets every live offer's conditional sentence (dates in the business time zone, end date inclusive) or, with none live, the owner's discount reply and an owner alert; the Business Folder page warns about an offer live over 90 days with no end date.
  3. While a draft waits, an easy answer that does not touch it sends on its own and the draft stays live with "The customer wrote again since this draft"; a message that touches it replaces the draft, with any split-off FAQ or intake answer sent alongside the partial holding line; scheduling never sends while a scheduling draft waits.
  4. A customer who writes while a draft waits always gets a reply: the check-in reply (which alerts the owner) or the added-info reply; a price draft answered by a price sentence closes as `answered` and refuses Send with "Already answered".
  5. Holding replies use only fill-ins computed from `hours.md`; the check-in time is off by default; every new reply the folder needs is required at sync, and the example folder passes with all of them.
