## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Internet -> POST /leads | Unauthenticated public form posts (any website, any bot) |
| Form input -> contact records | Customer-supplied phone/email can collide with existing contacts |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-16 | Denial of service | POST /leads flooding | medium | mitigate | Named limiter `leads` (10/minute/IP, 429 tested) and field max lengths in StoreLeadRequest |
| T-01-17 | Tampering | Contact hijack via form (overwrite someone's phone or email) | high | mitigate | D-13: a form never overwrites a stored phone or email; conflicts become timeline notes (tested) |
| T-01-18 | Tampering | Mass assignment from the form | medium | mitigate | InboundData built from `validated()` field by field; #[Fillable] allow-lists on models |
| T-01-19 | Tampering | CSRF exemption scope | medium | mitigate | Exemption limited to the `leads` and `webhooks/*` URIs (getExcludedPaths test); the panel keeps CSRF |
| T-01-20 | Repudiation | Attribution rewritten by later touches | low | mitigate | First-touch fields set only at contact creation; each touch's cost kept on its activity (D-14, tested) |
| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | No package is installed by this plan |
</threat_model>
