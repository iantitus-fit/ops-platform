<objective>
Write a readable copy of what happened into `records/`: every inbound text or form re-renders that customer's card from the database and updates `customers/INDEX.md` and `open-leads.md`; `records/CLAUDE.md` routes the owner's AI; `php artisan records:rebuild` regenerates everything. Renders never lose an update when events arrive together.

Purpose: RECS-01 is how the owner's own AI reads the business by routing instead of querying a database (SPEC "The bridge", DEC-03). Cards hold customer-written text, so prompt-injection hygiene is part of the contract.
Output: `QueueCustomerRecordRender`, `RenderCustomerCard`, `CustomerCard`, `RecordsWriter`, `MarkdownText`, `records:rebuild`, the tracked `records/CLAUDE.md`.
</objective>
