## Flagged assumptions

- A reminder's lead is elapsed hours ("how long before the start", D-29), so across a daylight-saving change a 24-hour reminder lands at a different wall time than the visit; only the move to the window's end uses wall-clock days.
- When two reminders collapse, the kept row carries the larger lead (it fires once either way).
- A hand booking whose confirmation would wait until after the visit has started gets no confirmation row (D-28: skipped only if the appointment has started by then).
