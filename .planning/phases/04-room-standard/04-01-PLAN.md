Most of the edits are a few lines each: one constant, one regex branch, one match arm, two never-throwing readers, one binding, one panel call, a fixture and two tiny test classes. The estimate (90k) is within the plan budget. So Task 1 stays one verifiable slice, and Task 2 holds everything that is not on the path.
