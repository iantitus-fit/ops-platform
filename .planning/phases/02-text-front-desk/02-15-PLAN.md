## Trust Boundaries

| Internet -> /webhooks/text/twilio and /status | Public, CSRF-exempt routes; the signature is the only authentication |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-50 | Spoofing | Forged inbound or status webhook | high | mitigate | ValidateTwilioSignature on both routes; configured URL, hash_equals, 403; routes 404 unless the Twilio driver is on |
| T-02-51 | Tampering | Replayed webhook | medium | mitigate | Unique (provider, provider_message_id) plus the dispatched_at claim; status updates forward-only |
| T-02-52 | Information disclosure | Auth token or bodies in logs | medium | mitigate | Mismatch logs the host only; send errors carry status and Twilio code, never the token; body logging unchanged (local only) |
| T-02-53 | Denial of service | Every webhook rejected behind a proxy (URL mismatch) | low | mitigate | Signature over the configured URL with and without the default port (Pitfall 13) |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | accept | No package is installed; twilio/sdk was considered and not added (research) |
</threat_model>
