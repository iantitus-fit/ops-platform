| T-02-38 | Denial of service | Auto-responder ping-pong and model cost | medium | mitigate | Reply cap 6 per hour and 20 per day checked before the agent runs; pause and alert at the cap (D-52) |
| T-02-39 | Repudiation | Who took over or handed back | low | mitigate | paused_by and agent_paused / agent_resumed timeline entries |
| T-02-40 | Tampering | Customer text forging a request for a person to escalate | low | accept | A false handoff costs the owner one tap; it only alerts and pauses |
