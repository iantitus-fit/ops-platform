Jev wire contract (RESEARCH "Jev" section and docs/phase-2-owner-input.md section 5, confirmed against docs.typesafe.ai):
- Request: `POST {base_url}/systemone`, headers `Authorization: Bearer <key>`, `Content-Type: application/json`, `Accept: application/json`; body `{"model": "<pinned>", "state": {"customer_message": "...", "draft_reply": "..."}, "questions": {"intent": {"type": "choice", "instructions": "...", "criteria": {"other": "...", ...}}, "price": {"type": "noul", "instructions": "..."}, ...}}`. Question keys are letters, digits and underscores.
- Response: `{"model": "typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917", "answers": {"intent": {"type": "choice", "choice": "faq", "probabilities": {...}, "confidence": 0.81}, "price": {"type": "noul", "noul": 0.02}, ...}, "usage": {...}}`. A noul answer has no confidence. Read by `type` when present; tolerate a missing `type` when the key's shape matches the question asked.
