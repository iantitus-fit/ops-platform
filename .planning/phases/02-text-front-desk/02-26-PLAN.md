Send: `POST https://api.twilio.com/2010-04-01/Accounts/{AccountSid}/Messages.json` form `To`, `MessagingServiceSid`, `Body`, `StatusCallback`; response `sid`; 21610 is HTTP 400 with JSON `code` 21610 (read defensively). Status callback form: `MessageSid` (or `SmsSid`), `MessageStatus`, `ErrorCode` when failed or undelivered; callbacks can arrive out of order; answer 200.
