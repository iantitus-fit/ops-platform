## Accomplishments

- Third net (D-45): `AgentReply::$possibleDanger` is read as exactly `true` (a missing, null, "yes" or 1 reads false), the schema requires `possible_danger`, and the prompt tells the agent to set it for gas, fire or smoke, carbon monoxide or its signs near a furnace, sparking and water near electricity, and whenever it is not sure.
- When the flag is true, `RunFrontDesk::handle()` sends the safety line (hazard `general`, trigger `agent`) right after the agent answers, unless a line already answers a text in the burst. The owner's sentences move into `heldSentences`, the draft is held as `agent:possible_danger` (words, a blank line, the sentences), no holding reply follows, the conversation is paused and the owner has a `life_safety` alert. Dana's thread has the safety line and nothing else.
- `possible_danger` with `wants_person`: only the safety line goes out; the owner gets an `asked_for_person` alert (and a complaint alert if the reading found one); no handoff text is sent into a paused conversation.
- D-51: after `ReportFrontDeskFailure` on the no-agent and every-model-failed returns, `replyWhenFrontDeskCannotAnswer()` sends `SendFixedReply::holding()`. Jev up or down, Dana gets exactly one holding reply and the owner the `front_desk_failed` alert; a second text while the models are still down gets no second reply but its own alert.
- D-45 on those paths: a complaint reading sends the complaint reply (kind `complaint_holding`) instead, and raises a complaint alert through `RaiseOwnerAlert`, so it goes at once under `right_away` and waits for 2026-10-14 08:00 America/Chicago when complaint is off the list, while `front_desk_failed` keeps its own timing.
- The crash path (`ProcessInbound`'s catch and `failed()`) is unchanged and still texts the customer nothing.
