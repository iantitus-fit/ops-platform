## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **Send ignores the "customer wrote again" signal** (`conversation-thread.blade.php:13-15`, `:34`). The badge is a `<p>` above the draft box; Send is unchanged and has no `wire:loading`/`wire:target`, so on a phone the owner can tap Send on a stale reply, or double-tap it. Fix: when `customerWroteAgain` is true, label the button "Send anyway" or add `wire:confirm`; add `wire:loading.attr="disabled"` to both Send buttons and `wire:confirm` to Discard (Phase 02 fix 1, carried).
2. **The Needs you card has no hierarchy** (`conversation-thread.blade.php:3-30`). Up to five unranked rows: hold reason, not-alerted badge, customer text, wrote-again badge, "The assistant wrote". The most urgent signal (the draft may answer a stale message) looks like routine info. Fix: move the wrote-again badge under the heading with a warning icon, give the section `color="warning"` and an icon, and when `customerWroteAgain` is true quote the customer's newest text instead of `lastCustomerMessage`.
3. **The Offers warning uses hardcoded red and no actionable detail** (`business-folder.blade.php:56`; text built at `BusinessFolder.php:181`). `style="color: #dc2626"` ignores dark mode and Filament's palette (Phase 02 fix 3 again), and a stale offer is a warning, not an error. The sentence shows a raw ISO date and never names the file to edit. Fix: `<x-filament::badge color="warning">` or `text-warning-600 dark:text-warning-400`; name the offer file; format the date "M j, Y" like the rest of the page.

## Minor Recommendations

4. "Draft answered" lives on the agent-facing markdown card (`CustomerCard.php:164`). Check it also shows on the owner's activity timeline; otherwise "Already answered" appears only as a danger toast. Consider saying who answered and when.
5. The wrote-again flag arrives by `wire:poll.5s`, possibly while the owner is typing, with no `aria-live`. Add `role="status"` to the badge paragraph, and check on a real phone that the poll does not reset the textarea (Phase 02 fix 6, carried; the phone walk is already WINDOWS.md entry 11).
6. Two hold reasons read nearly alike to an owner: "Held: waits behind a question you already have" and "Held: adds to a question you already have" (`Verdict.php`). Consider wording that says what to do.
7. Keep the pattern of the new price and discount reasons ("no price on file for this question", "no offer running today", service names via D-40); they are good.
8. The draft and reply textareas still have no `aria-label`, and three buttons are all labelled "Send" for different actions (Phase 02 fix 4, carried).

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)
Strengths:
- Hold reasons name the cause in plain words, and the wrote-again line says exactly what happened.
- The example SMS wording is warm, short and first-person ("Thanks. I've passed this to {owner_name} and you're on his list."). The complaint reply is the one place with an apology. Times come from `{today_or_next_open}`, so they are never wrong.
- The folder file explains each reply and its fill-ins in plain words.

Weaknesses:
- "Send" appears three times with no context.
- The Offers warning is generic (fix 3).
- The example replies hardcode "he"/"his" for the owner. That is fine for the fictional example owner (your plan-review wording), but the folder docs could point out that a business owner who uses other pronouns should edit those words.
- `discount_reply` says "I'll make sure {owner_name} knows" where the other texts say "I've passed", so the tense is inconsistent. It promises nothing, which is correct.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (2/4)
- The wrote-again badge is a small warning pill between two paragraphs and is the only cue that the draft may be stale. Its placement above Send matches D-22.
- The Offers section is a plain heading and red text, with no icon and no link to the file.
- The Needs you card is still `compact`, with no colour or icon (Phase 02, unfixed).

### Pillar 3: Color (2/4)
- `#dc2626` appears at `conversation-thread.blade.php:19`, `:63` and `business-folder.blade.php:25`, `:56`, `:84`; only `:56` is new.
- Bubble backgrounds are `rgba` literals. The amber `warning` colour is shared by the Front desk bubble and every warning badge (Phase 02, still true).
- The new badge correctly uses Filament's `color="warning"` rather than a hex value.

### Pillar 4: Typography (3/4)
- Only inline `0.875rem` and default sizes, `<strong>` for weight; nothing new. A stale offer warning is body size under a default section heading, so it reads like a note.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (2/4)
- New markup uses inline `style` attributes (`display: flex; gap: 0.25rem`) rather than Tailwind or Filament utilities; the inline-style count grew from Phase 02's 32. Gap values (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1rem) have no clear scale.
- The thread's `max-height: 60vh` is unchanged; with a phone keyboard open the thread area is small (not verified visually).

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (2/4)
Strengths:
- Wrote-again logic compares against the newest `in_reply_to` id with an `exists()` query, so a text that arrived mid-run still counts and a draft with no `in_reply_to` shows nothing. The flag is cleared on refusal and on resolution, and every case is tested.
- Day 90 is quiet and day 91 warns, in the business time zone (tested).
- Refusals keep the owner's words and show a toast.

Weaknesses:
- The stale-draft warning is advisory only, and Send and Discard have no loading, disabled or confirm states.
- The Offers warning has no action: no link to the file and no Sync button on the section.
- "Answered" is not a distinct visible state in the owner UI, only a toast.
- 375px mobile was not verified; plan 02.1-10 carried the phone walk to UAT.
