# Phase 02.1 Plan 24: The Reading on Every Automated Send Summary

**OutboundSender is now the second chokepoint: no automated text leaves without a reading of the customer's text in its gate, each part of a text is checked against what D-49 says it needs, and a send with no reading (or a missing one) alerts the owner at once with the new safety_check_missing.**

- **Tasks:** 3

## Accomplishments

- `OutboundSender::deliver()` refuses an automated text whose `gate.customer_reading` is absent, malformed or marked missing, throws `DomainException(NO_READING . why)`, queues nothing, and raises `safety_check_missing` for the conversation's newest customer text: always urgent, sent at once at any hour (tested at Wed 02:00 with `front_desk_failed` off `right_away`), and only once per customer text. The safety line (`SendOrigin::LifeSafety`) and the owner's own sends are untouched.
- Per-part rules (D-49): fixed lines need only the reading record, and a failed reading counts; owner price and offer sentences need `CustomerReading::allowsOwnerSentences()` (ran, did not fail, no complaint); model words need `gate_outcome` send, `checks.classifier.ran` and `reading.sendable` true, and a reading that did not fail. A part that falls short raises `front_desk_failed` on the folder's `owner_alert` timing (stored for 08:00 after hours when off `right_away`), never `safety_check_missing`.
- `SendFixedReply::holding()` / `handoff()` and `SendCombinedReply::handle()` take the run's `CustomerReading` and put it in the gate they build; `RunFrontDesk` passes it to every holding, handoff, code answer, partial holding, check-in and added-info send. Model-word sends already carried it through the verdict's gate.
- `RunFrontDesk` raises `safety_check_missing` right after the reading is taken when it is missing, before any send is tried. With no live folder, through the fake webhook and the queued job: exactly one alert whose text names that no business folder is live, Jev stays up (no outage, no `safety_check_down`), nothing is sent to the customer.
- `AlertReason::SafetyCheckMissing` ("Safety check did not run"); `RaiseOwnerAlert::isUrgent()` forces it, with `LifeSafety` and `SafetyCheckDown`; `handle()` gained no parameter.
- The six while-down rows (D-50) and the D-49 holding-reply test were added to `JevDownTest`; all already held from plans 02.1-17, 18, 21 and 23.

## Ian's decision, 2026-10-16: a missing reading counts as no reading at all

A reading marked missing (plan 02.1-23: the `RunFrontDesk::reading()` fallback, or no live business folder) is treated as no reading. The run raises `safety_check_missing` at once, as soon as it finds the reading missing and before any send is tried, one per customer text through the existing duplicate check; for the no-folder case the alert's reason names that no business folder is live. `OutboundSender`'s no-reading check treats a stored missing reading like an absent one (`safety_check_missing`, never `front_desk_failed`). Model words hold, nothing model-written sends, and Jev stays marked up. Implemented by `CustomerReading::fromStoredIfMade()` (a missing record reads back as no reading), `RunFrontDesk::alertAboutMissingReading()` and the sender check.
