#### Weighing the review warnings against the goal

- **WR-01 (back-up alert starts "Safety check is down:"): advisory, does not block.** The goal is about customer answers: price and offer sentences, easy answers beside a waiting draft, and a waiting customer always hearing back. The back-up alert is owner-facing and is delivered at once; its body says "The safety check is back" and gives the count, so the owner is not misled about the state, only given a contradictory lead. It cannot change what any customer is sent. It is a real defect in the one text meant to calm the owner at 3 am, nothing pins it in a test, and the fix is a few lines (a title argument or a `SafetyCheckBack` reason plus one assertion). Fix it in the next quick task or during the 02.2 alert rebuild; WINDOWS entry 16 is where Ian will see it on a phone.
- **WR-03 (blank promise-labelled words, held price, loses `label:promise`): advisory, does not block.** I traced it: with blank words, a promise label and a price that yields no sentence, `holdWithoutWords` records `price:no_sentence` and `checks.label.passed = null`, and `priceAlone()` only consults `checks.label.label` when `passed === false`. So the draft can later be closed as answered by a sentence for the same open service, with no new alert. Three things keep it off the blocker list. First, the model wrote no words, so no promise reached the customer; the label flags words that do not exist. Second, the owner was already alerted when the draft was stored, the customer got the owner's holding reply, and the customer reading (complaint, danger, person) is still required to be clean for any close, so the CR-02 class of loss (a complaint or hazard nobody read) is not reachable. Third, the close needs a later text whose sentence covers the same open slug. It is a small risk to a non-safety flag, and it does not break "a customer who is waiting always hears back". Fix it with the one-line change in the review (keep the code-hold reason whenever there is one, and have `priceAlone()` refuse a never-on-its-own label other than price) plus the review's test, before 02.2 builds the "handled" follow-up on top of auto-closed drafts.
- **WR-02 (`possible_danger` fail-open):** advisory. The structured schema marks the field required, Jev's reading and the keyword list both run before it, and it is the third net. Worth the two-line fix because a fallback provider that ignores the schema, with Jev down, would drop it silently.
