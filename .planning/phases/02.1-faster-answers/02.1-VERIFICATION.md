**CR-02.** Script: 6 pm (closed). "Your tech was rude last time and I am furious. How much for a new unit?", model replies "Mike will text you a price..." labelled `price`.
Result: draft W held, `checks.classifier = {ran:false, reading:null}`, one `draft_waiting` alert. Next text "It's the AC" with a blank reply, `price_services: ['ac-repair']`, `touches_waiting: true`.

### Gaps Summary

Two blocker gaps, both from one root cause: the phase let "code wrote it" skip the checks that read the customer's text.

1. **CR-01 (code-only answer skips the complaint check).** A pure price or discount text goes out as a sentence without Jev or the model's label being consulted. A complaint inside that text is answered with a price and never reaches the owner. Fix: block `codeOnly` on a complaint or promise label, and have Jev read the customer text hold-only on blank-word runs.
2. **CR-02 (answered close trusts a missing reading).** `priceAlone()` counts an unrun classifier as agreement, so a draft held at the label veto can be auto-closed as answered and its alert dropped even when the customer's text held a complaint. Fix: require `classifier.ran` with a non-null reading before closing, and make the label-veto and blank-word holds record a Jev reading.

Suggested closure plan: one plan for both (they share the "Jev reads the customer text on every hold" fix), with the four missing tests from WR-07 as its tests-first task. WR-01 and WR-04 are worth folding into the same plan since they touch the same branches of `RunFrontDesk`. After closure, re-run the two probes above as permanent tests.

Passing evidence worth keeping: 1455 of 1455 tests on SQLite (this run) and PostgreSQL 17 (plan 02.1-16 run), 36 of 36 security threats closed, VALIDATION nyquist_compliant true. The suite did not catch CR-01 or CR-02 because no test combines a complaint in the customer text with a blank-words or label-vetoed run.
