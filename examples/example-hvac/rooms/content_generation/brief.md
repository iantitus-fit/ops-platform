# Content brief

## Who we write for

Homeowners in our service area who have central air, a heat pump or a furnace. They are busy and want to know what a visit involves before they call.

## Topics we cover

- Seasonal tune-ups: what they check and why spring and fall are the right times.
- What a diagnostic visit includes, step by step.
- Signs a system is near the end of its life.

## Topics we avoid

- Prices beyond the sentences already in the folder.
- Comparisons between brands.
- Anything about gas or carbon monoxide. That goes to the owner.
