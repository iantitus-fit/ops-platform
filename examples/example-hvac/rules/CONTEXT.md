# Rules

What the assistant may do on its own and what always waits for the owner.

- `sending.md`: the send-or-draft list and how the owner is alerted
- `replies.md`: the holding and handoff texts that go out without you, checked for prices, discounts and promises
- `triage.md`: the questions the safety check asks about every reply, and which kinds of message may ever send on their own

These rules are enforced by the system, not only by instructions.
