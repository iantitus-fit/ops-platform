## What does a diagnostic visit cost?

The diagnostic fee is $89. We waive it when the customer approves the repair during the same visit.

## Where do you work?

We serve Chicago, Evanston, Oak Park and Skokie. Anywhere else, ask the owner before promising a visit.

## Which brands do you service?

We service all major brands of central air, heat pumps, mini-splits and furnaces. We do not service window units.

## Do you come out after hours?

No. We do not make after-hours visits. A customer who texts at night gets the first opening the next business morning.
